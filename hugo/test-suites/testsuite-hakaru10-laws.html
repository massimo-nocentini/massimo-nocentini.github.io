Laws

The equational laws of model composition (P §2.1 l.250-281, §4.3 l.803-843): Dirac is the left and the right unit of composition, and an unused variable is marginalized. Under the MCMC engine the right unit law holds sample for sample (X2, X16); the left unit law and marginalization hold in distribution. Under exact enumeration, which walks the built graph and reuses no incremental machinery (plan §6.10), they hold exactly.

do {x ← p; diracN x} ≡ p,  do {x ← diracN v; p} ≡ let x = v in p

A5: pjoin samples the product of pbern and pnorm (P l.264-272; E:36-42): P(x) = 0.4 and x, y uncorrelated.

(define pjoin (do/monad (← x pbern) (← y pnorm) (return (pair x y))))

pjoin = do
  x <- pbern
  y <- pnorm
  return (pair x y)

_ = mcmC 100 pjoin
(define (test/laws/joint _)
  (let* ((samples (mcmC 10000 pjoin))
         (xs (map (λ (s) (if (car s) 1.0 0.0)) samples))
         (ys (map cdr samples))
         (mx (mean xs))
         (my (mean ys))
         (cov (mean (map (λ (x y) (* (- x mx) (- y my))) xs ys)))
         (corr (/ cov (sqrt (* (cdr (momenta xs)) (cdr (momenta ys)))))))
    (⊦ (equal-approx? 0.03) 0.4 mx)
    (⊦ < (abs corr) 0.05)))

test/laws/exact: pass

The laws under exact enumeration, where a model is its distribution: the right and left units, marginalization, and a Dirac node introduced for a lifted parameter (ph2, whose posterior is 0.58, E:91). Enumeration walks the built graph; it reuses no incremental machinery (plan §6.10): a Dirac node's value is recomputed from its parents' on each path.

(define (test/laws/exact _)
  (let ((coin (dist bern 0.4))
        (pmarg/coin
          (do/monad (← x (dist bern 0.4)) (← y (dist bern 0.3)) (return y))))
    (⊦ equal?
       (exact-posterior coin)
       (exact-posterior (do/monad (← x (dist bern 0.4)) (diracN x))))
    (⊦ equal?
       (exact-posterior (dist bern 0.3))
       (exact-posterior (do/monad (← p (diracN 0.3)) (dist bern p))))
    (⊦ (equal-approx? 1e-15)
       (exact-posterior (dist bern 0.3))
       (exact-posterior pmarg/coin))
    (⊦ (equal-approx? 1e-15)
       (exact-posterior ph2)
       (exact-posterior
         (do/monad
           (← x (dist bern 0.2))
           (← q (diracN (lift (λ (xv) (if xv 0.1 0.7)) x)))
           (dist bern q))))))
