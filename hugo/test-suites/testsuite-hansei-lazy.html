Correlated memoization and lazy permutations

Lazy lists, shared lazily-evaluated variables and memoized stochastic functions, after Correlated memoization and lazy permutations, slazy.ml and Realistic Example: sorted list:

❝
Sampling of permutations, stochastic bijective functions, sequences of correlated random variables, the all-different constraint -- are related to the same problem. A problem with so many names must be important, and so must be its efficient solution.

Oleg Kiselyov

The goal is a sequence of N all-different random variables over 0 .. N-1, a stochastic bijection: the naive f1 of LP:36-41, which draws uniformly_list [0;1] at every application, is not even a function (test test/correlated); probcc-memo makes it one, and probcc-lazy-permutation (LP:127-229) and probcc-lazy-permutation/ins (LP:266-330) correlate the memoized values, so that each one is drawn only when asked for, among the values still free.

Laziness and non-determinism

Laziness and non-determinism make a tricky combination (SL:10-93): a variable bound with let is computed in every world, also where it is not needed; a thunk is not shared; and the built-in delay is shared across worlds, which gives a wrong distribution (DSL p.21 l.912-922). probcc-letlazy evaluates its body at most once per world, when it is first needed (P:270-330).

Realistic example: sorted list

Harik's problem (H #sorted, SO:1-11) draws N numbers uniformly from 0 .. M-1 and asks for the probability that they are sorted; with numbers as lazy lists of bits, the comparison stops at the first differing bit, which is crucial:

❝
Harik's problem is ideal for a benchmark: it is easy to explain and to model. The expected result is also known. Getting any useful estimate of the result is rather difficult, especially without extensive fine-tuning of the inference algorithm.

Oleg Kiselyov

The exact probability for four numbers of five bits, over 2^20 worlds, and upstream's importance-sampling runs of it are checked in test/sorted.
