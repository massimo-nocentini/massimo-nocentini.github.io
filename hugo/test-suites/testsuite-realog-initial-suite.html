test/General: pass

define-predicate derives the name of a relation from the bare words of its header, dashed together, so (: x string?) is (: α) for (: β) (: y string?) defines is-for° of four arguments: the words carry the meaning and the holes the data, which is how a multi-word infix predicate of the original survives the translation. Two headers agreeing on their words would name one relation, is-for° and is-for-in° differing by the single in.

A rule is a tuple of the relation followed by :- and the goals that establish it, hence "P4", declared for is-for° alone, reaches is-for-in° through the one rule: of the three answers two are declared and the third derived.

The verb is a hole here too, (: α), so Father, Grandfather, Brother and Cousin are values of an argument rather than four relations, and the whole scope becomes the one ternary is-of° that answers thirty-four times.

Its artefacts are the instructive part. Six of the ten Brother rows make somebody his own brother, exactly the six people the table gives a father to, the rule asking for a shared father and never for the two to differ; Emil, whose own father it does not record, is absent from them. Cousin repeats the pattern, five of its thirteen rows being reflexive, exactly those with a recorded grandfather. Marking a hole ≠° is what excludes them in the genealogy suite.

define-knowledge supersedes define-predicate: it names a scope, prefixes every relation with it, registers each in the scope's hash table — two of them here — and defines a macro of the scope's own name, so a query may be written in the word order of the original, (genealogy :a is Grandfather of :b), and answer exactly as the derived name does.

The body of a fact is tagged by the form its tuples arrive in, text/plain for those written out; the sketches beside it name sources that remain unimplemented.

The holes read (:a string?) and (:a string), a predicate and a type where a hole is now read as naming a relation, hence they declare nothing that is enforced. (:a plot-person°) of the genealogy suite is the form that binds.
