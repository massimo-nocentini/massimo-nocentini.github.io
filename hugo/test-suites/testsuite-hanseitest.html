Introduction

Quoting from Embedded domain-specific languages for probabilistic programming:

❝
Broadly speaking, probabilistic programming languages are to express computations with degrees of uncertainty, which comes from the imprecision in input data, lack of the complete knowledge or is inherent in the domain. More precisely, the goal of probabilistic programming languages is to represent and automate reasoning about probabilistic models, which describe uncertain quantities -- random variables -- and relationships among them.

Oleg Kiselyov

Here we focus on the dsl Hansei Embedded probabilistic domain-specific language HANSEI and the corresponding published paper Embedded Probabilistic Programming, In proceedings of the IFIP working conference on domain-specific languages, ed. Walid Taha. LNCS 5658, Springer, 2009, pp. 360-384., by Oleg Kiselyov and Chung-chieh Shan; moreover, an application to logic can be found in HANSEI as a Declarative Logic Programming Language.

Implementation

The first implementation of our language uses the probability monad that represents a stochastic computation as a lazy search tree. That is, our implementation uses the type constructor pV defined below. 

type 'a vc = V of 'a | C of (unit -> 'a pV) and 'a pV = (prob * 'a vc) list
Each node in a tree is a weighted list of branches. The empty list denotes failure, and a singleton list [(p, V v)] denotes a deterministic successful outcomevwith the probability mass p. A branch of the form V v is a leaf node that describes a possible successful outcome, whereas a branch of the form C thunk is not yet explored. The intended meaning of a search tree of type 'a pV is a discrete probability distribution over values of type 'a.

    (cond ((not (integer? n))
           (probcc-signal 'malformed 'probcc-uniform "not an integer count" n))
          ((> n 0)
           (letrec ((p (/ 1 n))
                    (plus (op/plus))
                    (subtract (op/subtract))

                             (if (zero? i)
                               (probcc-distribution
                                 (cons `(,i ,(subtract 1 pacc)) acc))
                               (loop (plus pacc p) (cons `(,i ,p) acc) (sub1 i))))))
             (loop 0 '() (sub1 n))))
          (else (probcc-impossible))))

    (letrec ((times (op/times))
             (subtract (op/subtract))

                            (else (times x (expi x (sub1 n)))))))

                        '()

                              (loop (times (subtract 1 p) pp) (add1 i)))))))

  (define (probcc-explore maxdepth choices)
    (letrec ((times (op/times))
             (plus (op/plus))
             (loop (λ (p depth down choices ans susp)
                      (match/first
                        choices
                        (() (cons ans susp))
                        ((((V ,v) ,pt) unquote rest)
                         (loop p
                               depth
                               down
                               rest
                               (pmap-insert-with plus v (times pt p) ans)
                               susp))
                        ((((C ,t) ,pt) unquote rest)
                         (cond (down
                                (let1 (down* (or (not maxdepth) (< depth maxdepth)))
                                      (match/first
                                        (loop (times pt p)
                                              (add1 depth)
                                              down*
                                              (t)
                                              ans
                                              susp)
                                        ((,ans* unquote susp*)
                                         (loop p depth down rest ans* susp*)))))
                               (else
                                (let1 (susp* (cons `((C ,t) ,(times pt p)) susp))
                                      (loop p depth down rest ans susp*)))))))))
      (match/first
        (loop 1 0 #t choices pmap-empty '())
        ((,ans unquote susp)
         (probcc-sort/mass (probcc-pmap->pv ans identity susp))))))

  (define (probcc-nan-inside? x)
    (cond ((number? x) (or (nan? (real-part x)) (nan? (imag-part x))))
          ((pair? x)
           (or (probcc-nan-inside? (car x)) (probcc-nan-inside? (cdr x))))
          ((vector? x)
           (let loop ((i 0))
             (and (fx< i (vector-length x))
                  (or (probcc-nan-inside? (vector-ref x i))
                      (loop (fx+ i 1))))))
          ((record-instance? x) (probcc-nan-inside? (record->vector x)))
          (else #f)))
  (define (probcc-structural=? a b)
    (and (fx= 0 (pmap-compare a b)) (not (probcc-nan-inside? a))))
  (define (probcc-prob-of v pv)
    (let loop ((pv* pv))
      (match/first
        pv*
        (() 0)
        ((((V ,w) ,p) unquote rest)
         (if (probcc-structural=? w v) p (loop rest)))
        ((,_ unquote rest) (loop rest)))))
  (define (probcc-at-least prob v pv) (>= (probcc-prob-of v pv) prob))
  (define (probcc-map-dist f pv)
    (let* ((plus (op/plus))
           (pm (fold (λ (e pm)
                        (match/first
                          e
                          (((V ,v) ,p) (pmap-insert-with plus (f v) p pm))
                          (else (probcc-signal
                                  'malformed

                                  e))))
                     pmap-empty
                     pv)))
      (pmap-foldi (λ (v p a) (cons (probcc-value p v) a)) pm '())))

  (define probcc-nearly-one (- 1.0 9.9999999999999995e-08))
  (define (probcc-explore/shallow maxdepth choices)
    (letrec ((times (op/times))
             (plus (op/plus))
             (loop (λ (pc depth ans acc lst)
                      (match/first
                        lst
                        (() (cons ans acc))
                        ((((V ,v) ,p) unquote rest)
                         (loop pc
                               depth
                               (pmap-insert-with plus v (times p pc) ans)
                               acc
                               rest))
                        (((,c unquote rest) ⊣ (>= depth maxdepth))
                         (loop pc depth ans (cons c acc) rest))
                        ((((C ,t) ,p) unquote rest)
                         (match/first
                           (loop (times pc p) (add1 depth) ans '() (t))
                           ((,ans* unquote ch)
                            (let* ((ptotal (probcc-total plus ch))
                                   (acc* (cond ((zero? ptotal) acc)
                                               ((< ptotal probcc-nearly-one)
                                                (let1 (ch* (probcc-scale ch ptotal))
                                                      (cons `((C ,(τ ch*)) ,(times p ptotal)) acc)))
                                               (else (cons `((C ,(τ ch)) ,p) acc)))))
                              (loop pc depth ans* acc* rest)))))))))
      (match/first
        (loop 1 0 pmap-empty '() choices)
        ((,ans unquote acc) (probcc-pmap->pv ans identity acc)))))
  (define (probcc-first-success choices)
    (let1 (times (op/times))
          (let loop ((choices* choices))
            (match/first
              choices*
              (() '())
              ((((V ,_) ,_) unquote _) choices*)
              ((((C ,t) ,pt) unquote rest)
               (let1 (ch (probcc-map
                           (λ1-match/first
                             ((,slot ,p) (list slot (times pt p))))
                           (t)))
                     (loop (append rest ch))))))))
  (define (probcc-explore/bounded maxsize choices)
    (letrec ((times (op/times))
             (plus (op/plus))
             (loop (λ (explore? pc low high q qsize lst)
                      (match/first
                        lst
                        (() (next low high q qsize))
                        ((((V ,_) ,p) unquote rest)
                         (let1 (pe (times pc p))
                               (loop explore?
                                     pc
                                     (plus low pe)
                                     (plus high pe)
                                     q
                                     qsize
                                     rest)))
                        ((((C ,t) ,p) unquote rest)
                         (if explore?
                           (let1 (q* (pmap-insert-with append (times pc p) (list t) q))
                                 (loop explore? pc low high q* (add1 qsize) rest))
                           (loop explore?
                                 pc
                                 low
                                 (plus high (times pc p))
                                 q
                                 qsize
                                 rest))))))
             (next (λ (low high q qsize)
                      (if (zero? qsize)
                        (list low high)
                        (let1 (explore? (< qsize maxsize))
                              (match/first
                                ((if explore?
                                   pmap-delete-find-max
                                   pmap-delete-find-min)
                                 q)
                                (((,p ,t unquote ts) ,q*)
                                 (loop explore?
                                       p
                                       low
                                       high
                                       (if (null? ts) q* (pmap-add p ts q*))
                                       (sub1 qsize)
                                       (t)))))))))
      (loop #t 1 0 0 (pmap-create number-compare) 0 choices)))
  (define (probcc-sample-dist/rejection selector n choices)
    (letrec ((times (op/times))
             (plus (op/plus))
             (loop (λ (pcontrib ans ch)
                      (match/first
                        ch
                        ((((V ,v) ,p))
                         (pmap-insert-with plus v (times p pcontrib) ans))
                        (() ans)
                        ((((C ,t) ,p)) (loop (times p pcontrib) ans (t)))
                        (else (match/first
                                (selector ch)
                                ((,th ,ptotal)
                                 (loop (times pcontrib ptotal)
                                       ans
                                       (list (list th 1)))))))))
             (driver
               (λ (ans i)
                  (if (zero? i) ans (driver (loop 1 ans choices) (sub1 i))))))
      (let1 (ans (driver pmap-empty n))
            (when (probcc-verbose)
                  (printf "rejection_sample: done ~a worlds\n" n))
            (probcc-sort/mass
              (probcc-pmap->pv ans (λ (p) (probcc-ratio p n)) '())))))
  (define ((probcc-sample-runner/times n) z step)
    (let loop ((z* z) (i n))
      (if (zero? i) (list z* n) (loop (step z*) (sub1 i)))))
  (define (probcc-sample-dist selector runner choices)
    (letrec ((times (op/times))
             (plus (op/plus))
             (add (λ (v p ans) (pmap-insert-with plus v p ans)))
             (look-ahead
               (λ (pcontrib)
                  (λ (e answers)
                     (let ((ans (car answers)) (acc (cdr answers)))
                       (match/first
                         e
                         (((V ,v) ,p)
                          (cons (add v (times p pcontrib) ans) acc))
                         (((C ,t) ,p)
                          (match/first
                            (t)
                            (() answers)
                            ((((V ,v) ,p1))
                             (cons (add v (times (times p p1) pcontrib) ans)
                                   acc))
                            (,ch
                             (let1 (ptotal (probcc-total plus ch))
                                   (cons ans
                                         (cons (if (< ptotal probcc-nearly-one)
                                                 (list (probcc-scale ch ptotal) (times p ptotal))
                                                 (list ch p))
                                               acc)))))))))))
             (loop (λ (pcontrib ans ch)
                      (match/first
                        ch
                        ((((V ,v) ,p)) (add v (times p pcontrib) ans))
                        (() ans)
                        ((((C ,t) ,p)) (loop (times p pcontrib) ans (t)))
                        (else (match/first
                                (fold (look-ahead pcontrib) (list ans) ch)
                                ((,ans*) ans*)
                                ((,ans* unquote cch)
                                 (match/first
                                   (selector cch)
                                   ((,th ,ptotal)
                                    (loop (times pcontrib ptotal) ans* th)))))))))
             (toploop
               (λ (pcontrib ans cch)
                  (match/first
                    (selector cch)
                    ((,th ,ptotal) (loop (times pcontrib ptotal) ans th)))))
             (driver
               (λ (pcontrib vals cch)
                  (match/first
                    (runner pmap-empty (λ (ans) (toploop pcontrib ans cch)))
                    ((,ans ,ns)
                     (let1 (ans* (pmap-foldi
                                   (λ (v p ans) (add v (times ns p) ans))
                                   vals
                                   ans))
                           (when (probcc-verbose)
                                 (printf "sample_importance: done ~a worlds\n" ns))
                           (probcc-sort/mass
                             (probcc-pmap->pv
                               ans*
                               (λ (p) (probcc-ratio p ns))
                               '())))))))
             (make-threads
               (λ (pcontrib ans ch)
                  (match/first
                    (fold (look-ahead pcontrib) (list ans) ch)
                    ((,ans*)
                     (probcc-sort/mass (probcc-pmap->pv ans* identity '())))
                    ((,ans* (,ch1 ,p))
                     (make-threads (times pcontrib p) ans* ch1))
                    ((,ans* unquote cch)
                     (driver pcontrib ans* (reverse cch)))))))
      (make-threads 1 pmap-empty choices)))
  (define (probcc-sample-rejection/a selector n thunk)
    (probcc-sample-dist/rejection selector n (probcc-reify/0 thunk)))
  (define-syntax-rule
    (probcc-sample-rejection selector n body ...)
    (probcc-sample-rejection/a selector n (τ body ...)))

Reification and reflection

The model is ordinary Scheme code; a choice is a call to probcc-distribution, which captures its continuation up to the nearest resetcc and answers a list of branches, one thunk per value, each resuming the continuation with its value (DSL p.14 l.627-628). probcc-reify/0 runs a model under resetcc and so turns it into a lazy search tree (DSL p.15 l.637): 

let reify0 m = reset (fun () -> pv_unit (m ()))
The paper's direct reifier is what this port keeps, on the single delimiter of (aux continuation delimited), where probM.ml goes through a request type and its interpreter (deviation 2):
❝
We pay the price of embedding stochastic computations only when calling the two stochastic operators dist and reify0. The deterministic parts of a stochastic program run at full speed, as if no randomness were present and no inference were in progress.

Oleg Kiselyov and Chung-chieh Shan, DSL p.15 l.676-679

The lawn model of DSL p.12 l.539-546, for example, is a function of no arguments: rain and a sprinkler may wet the grass, which is observed wet, and (probcc-reify/exact/a lawn) answers rain at 0.2838 and no rain at 0.3219999999999999, before normalization (test/probcc/grass-model reifies the same body):

(define (lawn)
  (let* ((rain (probcc-coin 0.29999999999999999))
         (sprinkler (probcc-coin 0.5))
         (grass-is-wet
           (or (and (probcc-coin 0.90000000000000002) rain)
               (and (probcc-coin 0.80000000000000004) sprinkler)
               (probcc-coin 0.10000000000000001))))
    (probcc-when grass-is-wet rain)))

probcc-reflect is the inverse of probcc-reify/0 (DSL p.16 l.694-700): it chooses following a tree, so that reifying a reflected tree gives an equivalent tree, and reflecting a reified model expresses the same distribution (DSL p.17 l.728-730; test/reflect/inverse). Reifying a sub-model, flattening its tree with probcc-explore and reflecting it is variable elimination (probcc-variable-elimination, DSL p.17 l.748-771): flips_xor' 10 explores 9 trees of depth 2 instead of the 1024 leaves of flips_xor 10; memoizing the eliminated function on its arguments is bucket elimination (λ-probcc-bucket, DSL p.18 l.776-783; test/variable-elimination).

Exploration and its order

probcc-explore (I:11-28, DSL p.9 l.393-408) flattens a tree to a given depth, or to the end when the depth is #f or +inf.0 (OCaml's None): the values reached are collated, their masses summed, and the branches below the depth are kept as suspended entries. Upstream threads the answer, a persistent map, and the list of suspensions through its loop, and so does this port: a thunk forced by the explorer may choose in an enclosing model, whose every world then resumes the loop, so a mutable table would be shared by all of them (test/explore/persistent).

The answer is sorted by descending mass, with a stable sort of upstream's order (deviation 5): equal masses keep the values in descending pmap-compare order, the stand-in for OCaml's compare (deviation 6), then the suspended branches in reverse discovery order. So the order of the tables is deterministic (test/explore/order, test/uniform/edges).

World-local memory

Every world of a model has its own memory, which the lazy variables, the memo tables and the world-local references live in. It is probM's first-class store (P:40-75):

❝
The first-class storage is immutable and pure functional. Only the pointer to it is mutable. This is critical as different threads may have different views of the same `memory' and one thread's updating its memory should not affect other threads that share it.

Oleg Kiselyov, P:59-63

❝
We implement first-class stores as immutable maps, and associate one with each delimited continuation.

Oleg Kiselyov and Chung-chieh Shan, ML p.1 l.82-83

probcc-thread-local is that pointer, a persistent map from fixnum locations to values, changed only with set!: probcc-reify/0 starts the model in an empty memory and restores the caller's when the first segment returns (P:104-107); probcc-distribution and probcc-reflect read the memory before capturing the continuation, and every branch resumes in it (P:118-148). A parameter or a dynamic-wind would not do: aux's shift enters the dynamic-wind frames of the captured continuation again on every resume, so a value cached in one world would show in another. A parameterize inside a model is independent of the memory (test/parameterize/memory).

Laziness

Events take place in another order than they are observed, and a value drawn long before it is looked at duplicates the search tree in between (DSL p.19-20 l.858-865). probcc-letlazy (P:321-330) answers a thunk that evaluates its body at most once per world, when first called, keeping the value in the world-local memory: "lazy let insertion at the point of reification" (P:292-298). The built-in laziness cannot do it:

❝
It is incorrect to implement letlazy using OCaml's built-in laziness, because the latter uses the mutable state of the underlying hardware, which does not `snap back' when the inference procedure backtracks to an earlier point in the execution of the probabilistic program.

Oleg Kiselyov and Chung-chieh Shan, DSL p.21 l.912-915

So delay, force and δ are never used for model values. probcc-letlazy is not nesting-safe, as upstream: an inner reification starts with an empty memory, so a lazy variable of the outer world is evaluated again inside, and the effects are reordered (N:65-81). probcc-letlazy/nesting (P:342-370) stores the unevaluated body where it is created and lets an inner world ask its parent for it (test/letlazy, test/nested).

Memoization

probcc-memo (P:449-479) generalizes the lazy variables to stochastic functions:

❝
The function (memo f) :: a -> Prob b is then a proper function: applied to an argument, it gives a sample from its output distribution. When applied to the argument again, it gives the same sample. The memo facility is the generalization of letlazy: indeed, letlazy is the particular case of memo when the function to memoize has the argument unit.

Oleg Kiselyov, P:456-463

Its table lives in the world-local memory, so a memo must be made inside the reification that uses it (deviation 16; test/memo). The table of λ-probcc-bucket is global instead, shared by every inference, which is right only for a function that reads no world-local state (deviation 11).

Nested inference

An inference procedure is an ordinary function, so a model may call one, and the choices it makes are choices of the enclosing model:

❝
Because a random choice made by an inference algorithm is expressed with dist just like any other random choice, any inference procedure, such as the importance-sampling algorithm in §4, can reason about itself or any other inference procedure.

Oleg Kiselyov and Chung-chieh Shan, EPP p.4 l.356-360

An inner probcc-reify/0 pushes its own delimiter and an empty memory; the inner explorer forces its thunks in the extent of the outer model, so a choice directly inside them (probcc-dist-selector, a lazy variable asking its parent) captures the inner explorer's loop, which therefore threads persistent state, as every inference loop of the core does (plan §5.7; test/nested, test/nested/thresholds). EPP p.4 l.374-379 memoizes the outer biased "as usual" and uses it at the inner level; with probM's memo that raises Not_found, and only letlazy_nesting works, here as upstream.

Inference about inference

PE:69-77 asks how likely an estimate made by sampling is to be at least 0.3:

❝
Suppose we choose a coin as before, then estimate p by flipping the coin twice and dividing the count of true by 2 (that is, by using rejection sampling with count 2). What is the probability that our estimate is at least 0.3? It is 7/8, because the only way for us to estimate below 0.3 is to choose a fair coin and get false from it twice.

Oleg Kiselyov and Chung-chieh Shan, PE:70-75

With probcc-dist-selector, the sampler's random choices are choices of the outer model, which exact inference enumerates: the outer table is exactly 7/8 and 1/8 (test/nested/inference-about-inference).

Samplers and selectors

A selector chooses one entry of a list of (x p) entries and answers (x ptotal) (deviation 18). probcc-random-selector draws from OCaml's generator, which (aux hansei random) transcribes, and its seed re-initialises the one global state, as Random.init does (P:221-222), so seeded runs replay upstream's records bit for bit (see the suite of (aux hansei random)); probcc-dist-selector chooses through the enclosing model instead (P:238-241).

probcc-sample-dist/rejection (I:121-135) follows a path from the root to a leaf per sample. probcc-sample-dist (I:137-198) is importance sampling with look-ahead:

❝
The key is to explore the tree shallowly before committing to any random choice among its branches. Whenever this look-ahead comes across a branch that ends in failure, that branch is eliminated from the available choices. Similarly, whenever the look-ahead comes across a branch that ends in success, the successful result is registered as a sample, and that branch is again eliminated from the available choices.

Oleg Kiselyov and Chung-chieh Shan, DSL p.19 l.837-842

On the drunk coin of DSL p.18-19, the and of ten tosses of a coin lost with probability 0.9, rejection sampling never sees the ten heads, whereas importance sampling estimates their probability, 9.77e-14 exactly (test/drunk-coin), at 7e-14 from 5000 samples with seed 1 (the replays of the random suite). EPP p.6 l.547-590 gives the algorithm step by step.

Two CHICKEN hazards

Two properties of CHICKEN break models that resume a continuation more than once, which every model of these suites avoids (plan §5.6):

  1. map builds its result destructively (srfi-1's too), so a continuation captured inside its procedure must not be resumed: (probcc-reify/exact (map (λ (x) (probcc-coin 1/2)) '(1 2))) answers the single entry (#t #t #f #f #t #f) at mass 1, the four worlds sharing one list built in place, instead of four lists at 1/4. Models use probcc-map, left to right as OCaml's List.map (deviation 20); list-tabulate and filter-map are re-entrant but call their procedure from the last element to the first (test/map/reentrant);
  2. under csi, a continuation captured while the operands of a call with five or more operands are evaluated accumulates them when resumed, even with plain call/cc: (probcc-reify/exact (list 'a 'b 'c 'd (probcc-coin 1/2))) answers (a b c d #t) and (a b c d #t #f); quasiquote templates and constructors are calls too. Models bind every choice with let* first. Compiled code is not affected.

OCaml evaluates the components of a tuple and the arguments of a call from right to left, let x = e1 and y = e2 in and the scrutinee of match (e1, e2) with from left to right; the ported models sequence their choices with let* in that order, which the depth-bounded and the replayed oracles depend on (plan D19).
