Introductory examples and regression tests

The example models of Embedded probabilistic domain-specific language HANSEI, one section per example of the page, with upstream's own expected values: the lawn model of the papers, the regression tests of exactInfM.ml, which liken HANSEI's syntax to IBAL's, and the early collapse of worlds:

❝
Naively, we would have thought we needed to evaluate 2^10 of possible worlds: each (nested) flip splits the world. But as the result shows, we managed with only 10+1 worlds. The reason is that the algorithm is NOT tail recursive, and && is a _special_ form rather than a function.

Oleg Kiselyov

The models keep upstream's flonum probabilities, so that their tables are upstream's bit for bit where the masses are summed in upstream's order; where a model draws with uniform, whose masses are the exact 1/n here (deviation 7), or reflects a table that probcc-explore has sorted by mass (deviation 5), the masses agree to a relative 1e-9.

Colored balls

Example: colored balls:

❝
This is an elegant example of a model with indefinite number of objects whose `real' identity is unobservable. The example is a simplified version of population estimation. HANSEI's implementation of the example makes the case for memoization of stochastic functions.

Oleg Kiselyov

Blood types

Example: blood types:

❝
The hidden variables are the presence of genes A and B in the chromosomes inherited from the mother and the father. The observed variable is the blood type, which is a stochastic function of the genotype. A typical query asks for the distribution of blood types for a particular individual, possibly conditioned on the known genotype of some of the ancestors.

Oleg Kiselyov

❝

Multitarget tracking

Realistic Example: Multitarget tracking:

❝
We found it crucial to sample the state of an aircraft and the location of a false alarm lazily. If the x-coordinate of the detected aircraft does not match the x-coordinate of any observed blip, we immediately report the contradiction (of the possible world with the evidence). We do not need to compute the y-coordinate of the aircraft, let alone the locations of other aircrafts and false alarms.

Oleg Kiselyov

The ported models are the AILog one (blip_ailog.ml, memoized), the naive one without laziness (blip_naive.ml) and the lazy one with sequential Monte-Carlo (blip.ml).

❝
The payoff is that the same cognitive machinery that the hearer uses to decide what to do, by interpreting utterances, can also be used by the speaker to decide what to say, by predicting how potential utterances would be interpreted or misinterpreted by the hearer.

Early collapse of worlds (X:236-347): the and of ten flips needs 11 worlds, not 2^10, because and does not evaluate its second operand when the first is false; the xor of ten flips needs all 1024, unless the xor of the others is reified and reflected, collapsing its worlds into two, so that each level explores 4.

(define ((flips-xor n))
  (let loop ((n n))
    (if (= n 1)
      (probcc-coin 0.5)
      (let* ((r (loop (sub1 n))) (x (probcc-coin 0.5))) (not (eq? x r))))))

(define ((flips-xor* n))
  (let loop ((n n))
    (if (= n 1)
      (probcc-coin 0.5)
      (let* ((r (probcc-reflect (probcc-reify/exact (loop (sub1 n)))))
             (x (probcc-coin 0.5)))
        (not (eq? x r))))))

(define (cb-observed-color q c)
  (if (probcc-coin q) c (case c ((Blue) 'Green) ((Green) 'Blue))))

(define ((cb-model-nballs q obs))
  (let* ((nballs (probcc-uniform/range 1 9))
         (ball-color
           (probcc-memo (λ (b) (probcc-uniform/either '(Blue Green))))))

              (unless
                (eq? (cb-observed-color q (ball-color b)) (car obs))
                (probcc-impossible)))
        (loop (cdr obs))))
    nballs))
