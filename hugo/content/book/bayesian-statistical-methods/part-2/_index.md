Chapters 5 through 8 move from one-parameter models to multiparameter normal models, multivariate covariance matrices, and nested populations. Along the way, we discover that breaking strict conjugacy forces us beyond direct sampling and introduces Markov chain Monte Carlo through the Gibbs sampler, together with the diagnostics needed to trust it and the hierarchical shrinkage that lets small groups borrow strength from larger populations.
