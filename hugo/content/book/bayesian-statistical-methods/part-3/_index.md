The final four chapters assemble the tools of modern applied Bayesian modeling: normal linear regression and Bayesian variable selection via Zellner’s $g$-prior, the general Metropolis–Hastings algorithm for nonconjugate generalized linear and time-series models, linear and generalized linear mixed effects models for grouped regression, and latent Gaussian constructions (ordered probit, rank likelihood, and Gaussian copulas) for arbitrary ordinal data.
