tags = [ "matrices", 'riordan', 'canonical', 'hermite', 'jordan' ]
abstract = '''
This paper collects results about Riordan arrays in the framework of matrix functions; actually, the following methodology applies to any square matrix m×m with exactly one eigenvalue λ of algebraic multiplicity m∈N. Generalized Lagrange bases are used to construct Hermite polynomials that interpolate a family of functions; moreover, we show a parallel application of such functions via Jordan canonical forms and case studies are given.'''
